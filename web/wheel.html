Read the water on a wheel

No reading for the road in front of you? A wheel standing in the water is a gauge. This says who must not go in. It never says who may.

Whose wheel?

Mind

  • Do not walk or ride into water to look. Read a wheel you can see from where you stand.
  • Do not enter moving water at any depth.
  • The depths come from standard wheel sizes. Nobody has yet tested how well people read a wheel this way.
  • Nothing you choose here is sent anywhere.
